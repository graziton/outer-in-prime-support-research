Definition

Let n have decimal digits d1 d2 ... dk. Define

    T(n) = d1 dk d2 d{k-1} d3 d{k-2} ...

by reading alternately from the left and right ends. The sequence consists of
positive integers m not divisible by 10 such that m != T(m) and
rad(m) = rad(T(m)), where rad(m) is the product of the distinct prime divisors.
